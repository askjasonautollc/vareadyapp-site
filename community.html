VA Ready & Vet Ready in the Community

I built this for veterans, so I show up in person.

I’m a retired Army Chief Warrant Officer, and I built VA Ready with a team of fellow veterans and people who know the VA claims process inside and out. This is where you’ll find us off the screen, newest first.

Putting Veterans in the Air

We’re sponsoring five free discovery flights for veterans in the Fort Stewart, Waycross and Kings Bay area, in partnership with Runway One Nine Aviation in Waycross, Georgia. A real aircraft and a certificated flight instructor, on us.

I soloed for the first time this summer, and I want to share that with the veterans who earned it. The FAA’s MOSAIC rule, which began taking effect in October 2025, expanded the aircraft a sport pilot can fly. A discovery flight is where that path can start.

I’m a veteran, not a marketing company

No agency went looking for a veteran angle here. I served twenty years in the U.S. Army, retired as a Chief Warrant Officer, and went through the VA claims process myself.

I dug through scattered rules, made my own checklists, and figured out the steps the hard way. Then I built the tool I wish someone had handed me. Read my story.

I don’t do it alone. I built VA Ready with a team of fellow veterans and people with years of hands-on experience in the VA claims process, including helping other veterans through it. They keep the app honest about what claims really look like on the ground.

How we support veterans

Free accredited help comes first

I built the app to point veterans to accredited Veterans Service Organizations like the DAV, the VFW, and county veterans service offices. Their help is free. Find a VSO near you.

Free and private

The core tools are free, with no account. Your claim information stays on your phone. I don’t sell your data, I don’t take a cut of your claim, and nothing I charge is tied to your benefits.

I show up in person

I’d rather meet veterans where they already gather, in the stands and on the flight line, than chase them with ads.

Built from veteran feedback

Veterans send reports straight from the app. My team and I read them, check them against the primary sources, and ship the fixes. A lot of every update started as a message from a veteran.

Jason, CW2, U.S. Army (Retired)
