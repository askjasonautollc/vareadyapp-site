In the Community

We show up in person, not just on your phone.

VA Ready is a veteran-founded company. This season, that means Friday nights in Liberty County, Georgia, with our name on the board at Donell Woods Stadium.

Friday nights in Liberty County

We sponsor the Liberty County High School Panthers football team for the full 2026 season. Every home game, our name runs on the video board at Donell Woods Stadium.

Liberty County is home to Fort Stewart. A lot of the families in those stands are military families. Plenty of the parents have served, and plenty are serving now. Those are the people we built this for, so that is where we wanted to be.

We’d rather earn trust in the stands than buy it in an ad.

Built by a veteran, not a marketing company

VA Ready was not built by an agency that found a veteran angle. It was built by a retired U.S. Army Chief Warrant Officer who went through the VA claims process himself.

He dug through scattered rules, made his own checklists, and figured out the steps the hard way. Then he built the tool he wished someone had handed him. Read our story.

How we support veterans

Free accredited help comes first

The app points veterans to accredited Veterans Service Organizations like the DAV, the VFW, and county veterans service offices. Their help is free. Find a VSO near you.

Free and private

The core tools are free, with no account. Your claim information stays on your phone. We don’t sell your data, we don’t take a cut of your claim, and nothing we charge is tied to your benefits.

We show up in person

Liberty County is where we started. We would rather meet veterans where they already gather than chase them with ads.

Built from veteran feedback

Veterans send reports straight from the app. We read them, check them against the primary sources, and ship the fixes. A lot of every update started as a message from a veteran.

Free to download on iPhone and Android.

VA Ready on the App Store·Vet Ready on Google Play

Same app, two names: VA Ready on iPhone, Vet Ready on Android.

The app is educational. It does not give claims advice, legal guidance, or predict ratings, and it points veterans to free accredited representatives. VA Ready is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.
