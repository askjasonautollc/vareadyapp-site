Before you meet your VSO

Bring your DD214, any letters from VA, and a list of your conditions with when each one started and where you were treated. If you have not filed yet, ask about an intent to file: it can hold your start date for up to a year while you gather evidence (38 CFR 3.155). See what a VSO does and how to avoid claim sharks.

Meeting with a VSO in Fulton County? Build your claim file in VA Ready first: scan or import your records, label each one with the VA document type, and tie it to the condition it supports. Pro adds a VSO packet with an evidence index on the front page, so your rep can turn straight to each record.
