What the app does

The app is an information and organizing tool for veterans and service members. It helps you:

  • Estimate your combined VA disability rating and pay
  • Look up rating criteria, exam guides, benefits and filing guides
  • Find conditions and exposures linked to your job, bases and duties
  • Build and organize your claim, with symptom and medication logs and a Claim File of documents
  • Find accredited representatives and read or post reviews
  • Set reminders and export a claim summary

The app does not file or submit claims, connect to VA systems or act on your behalf. You file on VA.gov or through an accredited representative.
