VA Ready / Vet Ready

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers VA Ready on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows, and Vet Ready on Android. They are the same app, made by JDL Software LLC, a Georgia company. "We" means JDL Software LLC. "The app" means whichever version you use.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no logins.
  • What you enter stays on your device unless you choose to send it.
  • We never sell your data, and the app shows no ads.
  • You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you. We do it within 45 days.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. This includes:

  • Your profile: branch, job codes, service dates, bases, exposures, state, dependents and rating
  • Your claim: conditions, ratings, evidence checklists, notes and secondary links
  • Your symptom log, medication log, journal and exam questionnaire answers
  • Documents and photos you add to your Claim File
  • Buddy statements, reminders, Intent to File dates and back pay estimates

We can't see any of it. Some of it is health information. We are not a health care provider, and we never receive it.

What the app sends, and when

The app sends data in only these cases:

  • Reading app content. The app downloads rating criteria, pay rates, benefits, guides and other content from our database. These requests never include your name or your claim. Some say what the content is for, such as your state, your branch and job code, a condition you open, or words you search for. Like most web servers, our database host records each request: your IP address, the time, the app version and the content asked for.
  • Finding a VSO. If you search by town or ZIP code, the app sends what you typed. On iPhone and Android, if you tap Use My Location, the app sends your device's location once to find nearby offices. We do not store your location in our database.
  • Sending feedback. The app sends your message, the feedback type, your branch and job codes, whether you have Pro, the app version and your device model. If you add an email address, we use it only to reply to you.
  • Posting a VSO review. The app sends the office, your star rating, your comment, your branch, whether you have Pro, the app version, your device model and a random review ID. The random ID lets the app hide or block reviews. It is not tied to your name. Other app users can see your review and the details sent with it. If you report a review, the app sends the reason, any note you add and the app version.
  • Buying Pro. Apple, Google or Microsoft handles every purchase. We never see your payment details. The store tells the app whether you have Pro.
  • Backups and sharing. If you turn on backups, the app saves a copy to a place you pick, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a folder on your computer. We can't access it. When you share or export a file, you choose where it goes. On Android, if your phone's own Google backup is on, Google also backs up a few app settings, never your claim or documents.
  • Crash reports and store prompts. If you allow it in your device settings, Apple or Google sends us crash reports that do not identify you. The app may ask you to rate it, and the store handles your rating.

Nothing else leaves your device. Reminders are created on your device. On Android, the document scanner runs on your phone. The app has no push notifications, ad trackers or analytics tools.

How we use what we receive

  • Server records: to deliver content, keep the service secure and stop abuse. We also count how many devices use the app each day and which content they open. We look at totals, not at individuals.
  • Feedback: to answer you, fix bugs and plan features.
  • Reviews and reports: to show reviews to other veterans and to moderate them.

We do not use any data for advertising. We do not build profiles of users or try to identify you.

Who else handles data

We share data only with the companies that run parts of the service for us:

  • Supabase hosts our database and keeps its server records. Its network partner Cloudflare passes requests along.
  • Vercel hosts vareadyapp.com and gives us cookie-free visitor totals for the website.
  • Apple, Google and Microsoft run the app stores and process purchases. If you back up to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive, that company stores your backup in your own account.
  • Our email provider holds messages you send to support@vareadyapp.com.

These companies may use the data only to provide their service to us. They must protect it at least as well as this policy does. No one else may use or disclose your data for any reason without your consent. That includes data with names or details removed. We may disclose data if the law requires it, such as under a valid court order.

We never sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade user data for money or anything else. We do not share data with marketers, advertisers or data brokers. We do not share de-identified or combined data outside the companies listed above.

Data about other people

Some things you add may be about other people, such as a buddy statement or a document that mentions your family. That data stays on your device like the rest. Before you share or back it up, think about the people it mentions.

How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app. There are no accounts, so nothing we hold is tied to an account that could go dormant.
  • Server records: our database host deletes them after about 90 days.
  • Feedback: deleted once we handle it, and within 12 months at most.
  • Reviews and reports: kept while the office is listed, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Support email: kept up to 12 months after the conversation ends.

How to delete your data

  • On your device: delete the app. This removes everything the app stored. On Windows, uninstall the app, then delete any VA Ready folders left in your Documents and AppData folders. Also delete any backups you saved to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or another folder.
  • Data we hold: email support@vareadyapp.com and ask us to delete your data. Tell us how to find it, such as the email address you used, or the office and date of a review.

We will permanently delete 100% of the data we hold about you, including data that did not come from VA. We do this within 45 days of your request and email you when it is done.

Security

  • Every connection between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our database lets the app read app content and public reviews, and add feedback, reviews and reports. The app cannot read anyone's feedback or reports.
  • Your device's own security protects what the app stores on it, so use a passcode.

If there is a data breach

If someone gets unauthorized access to data we hold, we will tell affected users as soon as we can, and within the time the law requires. We will post a notice in the app and on this website, and email anyone who gave us an email address. The notice will say what happened, what data was involved and what you can do.

If the company is sold or closes

If JDL Software LLC is sold, merges or closes, we will tell you in the app and on this website before any data moves. The new owner must follow this policy. If they want to change it, you will get notice first, and you can:

  • Save your data with the app's backup and export tools
  • Ask us to delete the data we hold about you
  • Stop using the app and delete it

Children

The app is for U.S. veterans and service members. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child sent us data, email us and we will delete it.

Our website

vareadyapp.com uses Vercel Web Analytics. It counts page views, top pages, referring sites, country and device type. It also counts taps on our App Store and Google Play buttons. It uses no cookies, does not follow you to other sites and does not identify you.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and in the app and update the date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.
