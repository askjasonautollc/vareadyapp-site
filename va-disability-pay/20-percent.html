20% VA disability pay

A 20% VA disability rating pays the amounts below each month, tax-free. These are the 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025.

2027 estimate: about $369.50 a month for a veteran alone, after the 3.6% COLA announced October 14, 2026. The increase starts December 1, 2026, in the payment that arrives in early January. VA has not published its 2027 table yet. See every estimated 2027 amount →

20% VA disability monthly pay by dependents

Your situationMonthly (tax-free)
Veteran alone (no dependents)$356.66

VA only adds money for dependents once your rating reaches 30%. At 20% the amount is the same whether or not you have a spouse, children, or dependent parents.

With several conditions, VA combines them with its own formula rather than adding, and rounds to the nearest 10%, so a combined 75% pays at the 80% rate. The calculator shows where yours land.
