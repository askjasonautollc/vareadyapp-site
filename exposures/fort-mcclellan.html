Fort McClellan exposure

Fort McClellan, Alabama hosted the Army Chemical Corps and sat near industrial PCB and chemical contamination. Veterans who trained or served there may have multiple exposure pathways.

Who is covered

Exposures and service VA recognizes:

  • Fort McClellan, Alabama
    January 1, 1935 – May 20, 1999
    Service at Fort McClellan, AL 1935-1999 may have involved radioactive Cs-137/Co-60, chemical warfare decon training, fog oil, HC smoke, and off-post PCB contamination from Monsanto Anniston.

To see how these conditions combine with others, use the combined-rating calculator.

Documents VA asks for

  • DD-214: service period at Fort McClellan
  • Personnel records / unit assignment: confirms duty at Fort McClellan
  • Medical diagnosis + doctor's nexus statement: VA decides case-by-case
