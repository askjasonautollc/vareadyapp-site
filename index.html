See the 38 CFR criteria behind each rating

VA Ready shows the 38 CFR break points for all 765 conditions, every 38 CFR diagnostic code cross-referenced, so you can see why a condition sits at 30 and not 50, using the same criteria your rater reads.

This is an educational tool. It shows you the criteria and does the math. It does not predict what the VA will award you.

Building your claim in the app

  • Add conditions from the 15 most-claimed list or search the VA’s full rating schedule.
  • Select your estimated rating, or match your symptoms to the full CFR criteria Pro
  • The bilateral factor applies on its own for paired extremity conditions under 38 CFR 4.26. Eye and ear conditions stay single, with no left and right split.
  • What-If stacks a possible claim, such as a 30% secondary condition, onto your rating with the same rating engine and shows the new combined rating and payment.
  • TDIU and P&T math at the 100% pay rate. TDIU separates granted from applying for it, so a pending claim never prints as a rating you hold.
  • Most Special Monthly Compensation levels replace your schedular rate. SMC-K adds on top.
  • Bilateral flat feet counts as one rating, because 38 CFR rates it as a single condition covering both feet.

How it works

Enter your service history, rate each condition, check Claim Intel, attach evidence, and export the summary you use on VA.gov. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Service history by period

Record each period you served with its dates, its component and the MOS you held during it. The same MOS can belong to more than one period. The VA applies different rules to Active, Reserve and Guard time under 38 CFR 3.6, and a rep cannot apply them to one collapsed date range, so each export prints your service history as separate line items.

  • Still serving

    A Service Status control works out where you are from your separation date, and you can override it either way. The Filing tab then becomes a runway with five stages: get complaints into your service treatment record, chase down missing diagnoses, pull your records while you still have base access, the BDD window from 180 to 90 days out, and a standard pre-discharge claim under 90 days. Your service treatment record freezes the day you sign out.

  • BDD screener

    Five questions cover the situations the VA handles outside the BDD program, so you know which path is yours. BDD moves the wait to the months before you separate. The decision comes no faster.

  • Reserve and Guard periods

    Annual Training can service connect a disease. A drill weekend generally covers injury only. The free duty-status check on this site shows which rule applied to each period you served, under 38 CFR 3.6.

    Check your duty status →

  • Collateral duties

    Save the collateral duties you held, such as SERE, Auxiliary Security Force, Detainee Operations, Drill Sergeant and Airborne, and they print on your claim summary, VSO packet and exposure profile. A collateral duty can carry exposures and claimable conditions your primary MOS does not imply, and your VSO only asks about duties you list.

Conditions linked to your job and where you served

Claim Intel Pro

Claim Intel merges the conditions tied to your MOS, your PACT Act presumptives and your exposures into one ranked list.

  • Add secondary and prior roles if you held more than one MOS, and the conditions merge across each job.
  • PACT Act presumptive matching, with a presumptive-paths badge on each condition's evidence checklist.
  • Tap a condition to see why it is on your list: the source MOS, the exposures that surfaced it, and the citation.
  • Tap to add it to your claim. The app warns you about pyramiding before it lands.
  • 1,098 MOS, AFSC, rating and designator profiles across all six branches, 213 legacy codes included
  • 89 additional-duty profiles: Airborne, Ranger, Pararescue, Combat Controller, MARSOC, Navy SEAL, USCG MSRT, Space Force operators and more

Keep your evidence by condition

A rater and a C&P examiner assess frequency and severity over time, and one exam captures one day. VA Ready labels each document and keeps it with the condition it supports.

  • Evidence checklist

    Diagnosis, service records, nexus letter, buddy statements and DBQ review for each condition. Check them off as you gather them, and the claim readiness score shows your evidence completion percentage across all conditions.

  • Claim Journal Free

    The Claim Journal keeps a dated, searchable record of your timeline, evidence and conditions, including the nexus letter you gathered and never sent, with a yearly reminder to review it.

  • C&P exam answers Pro

    The rating criteria ask how you are on an average day and a bad one. Answer the questions at your kitchen table: how it started, how often flare-ups hit and how long they last, what a good day looks like. An examiner is not required to accept papers at the exam, so submit your answers on a VA Form 21-4138 beforehand if you want them in your file.

  • Buddy statements

    Samples show the format only, because the witness must use their own words, and list the qualities the Board of Veterans’ Appeals credited, with citations.

Peer-reviewed studies for each condition

Open a condition in My Claim to see its studies, with the journal, year, key findings and a PubMed link. The library holds 2,403 studies from PubMed and the NIH across 395 diagnostic codes, from PTSD, tinnitus, back and knee to the cancers presumptive under PACT and Agent Orange, and newer coverage of Parkinson’s disease, ALS, multiple sclerosis, sarcoidosis, asbestosis and TBI-related dementia. Filing

Based on your separation date, the app tells you where you are in the VA process and what to do next. You still upload on VA.gov yourself, or a free accredited VSO files for you.

  • Export Center New in 3.0 Pro

    Pick who the PDF is for: your VSO, your C&P examiner, your doctor for a nexus letter, or yourself to print. The app ticks the sections that person usually needs and builds one PDF with a cover page, a contents list and numbered pages, so a rep can turn to a section instead of shuffling paper. Sending documents out is part of Pro.

  • VSO Packet Builder Pro

    Your rep has twenty minutes and no view into your notes. Build one numbered PDF with your conditions and ratings, the criteria level you selected, where your evidence stands, presumptives from your bases, ships, aircraft and vehicles, your symptom log summary, your exam answers, and your journal with gathered-but-unsent evidence flagged. The cover lists each service period with its branch and component, so your rep can apply 38 CFR 3.6.

  • Filing timeline

    Your BDD window, presumptive period and days since separation, an 11-step claim status tracker from “Preparing” to “Decision Made”, action checklists for your filing phase, and C&P exam preparation covering what the examiner will ask.

  • Claim Navigator

    Tell the app where you are, and the Claim Navigator lists the steps from “getting ready to file” to “decision made” for a new claim, a secondary condition, a PACT Act exposure or a rating increase. Compare filing paths before you start, see the usual wait at each stage, and save or share the plan with your VSO.

Common questions

Is VA Ready affiliated with the Department of Veterans Affairs?

No. VA Ready (iOS) and Vet Ready (Android) are independent products and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. All rating criteria come from publicly available federal regulations (38 CFR).

Is the VA disability calculator free?

Yes. The combined-rating calculator with TDIU and P&T math, your claim list, federal benefits, the accredited VSO finder, and the daily symptom log are free with no account required. Pro unlocks the full 38 CFR rating criteria, all 50 states’ benefits, and Claim Intel.

Why is the app called VA Ready on iOS but Vet Ready on Android?

It’s the same app and the same data. Google Play requires a non-government-sounding name, so the Android release is named Vet Ready while the iOS release is named VA Ready.

Does VA Ready keep my personal information private?

Yes. Your profile, claim list, and reminders stay on your device. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync of personal data. Reference content such as rating criteria and benefits is fetched read-only over an encrypted connection.

What does the Exposure Calculator do?

You add the bases you served at and the ships, aircraft and vehicles you worked on, and it cross-references your service against the PACT Act, Camp Lejeune, AFFF/PFAS, K2, Agent Orange, and other presumptive registries to surface the conditions you may be eligible to claim, each linked to a primary source.
