What VA Ready does and does not do

VA Ready is a reference and calculation tool. It applies the VA's rating rules and combined-rating math to the conditions you enter and links every study to its source.

Rating math, fees, representation and data

Where the numbers come from
Your combined rating is the VA's own math. The rating criteria are the text of 38 CFR. The research is real studies with citations you can open and read.
What it costs
VA Ready never touches your claim money. It charges no contingency fee and takes no percentage of your back pay. Filing your own claim with the VA is free. VA Ready charges a flat price for Pro, or nothing for the free tier.
Who represents you
VA Ready is an informational tool, not a representative. It does not give legal advice, does not file on your behalf and is not affiliated with the VA. When you need representation, the app points you to free, VA-accredited Veterans Service Officers.
Where your data lives
Your profile, conditions and reminders stay on your phone. There is no account, no login and no cloud copy of your claim. VA Ready does not collect your data, so it has none to sell. Reference content loads read-only over an encrypted connection.

Where each number comes from

A wrong rating estimate sets the wrong expectations before you file. In VA Ready, every rating comes from the VA's published math, every criterion is the regulation text, and every study is a citation you can verify.

Common questions

Does VA Ready use AI to calculate my rating?

No. VA Ready is not a chatbot and does not generate answers. Your combined rating comes from the VA's own math, the criteria come from the actual text of 38 CFR, and the research citations are real studies indexed in public databases. Nothing is guessed or invented.

Does VA Ready take a percentage of my back pay?

No. VA Ready never touches your claim money. There are no contingency fees and no cut of your retroactive pay. The app is free to use, with an optional flat-price Pro upgrade. Filing your own claim with the VA is always free.

Is VA Ready a law firm or a claims agent?

No. VA Ready is an informational tool, not a representative. It does not file claims for you, does not provide legal or medical advice, and is not affiliated with the Department of Veterans Affairs. For representation, the app helps you find a free VA-accredited Veterans Service Officer.

What does VA Ready do with my personal information?

Your profile, claim list, and reminders stay on your device. There are no user accounts, no cloud sync of personal data, and nothing is sold. Reference content like rating criteria and benefits is fetched read-only over an encrypted connection.

How does VA Ready compare to AI claim tools like VetClaims.ai, VeteranAI, or ClaimDuty?

They solve a different problem in a different way. Paid AI claim-prep services generally ask you to upload your service and medical records, then use AI plus staff review to draft a claim package, usually for a fee. VA Ready is a reference and calculation tool: it does not read your records, does not generate claim language with AI, and does not charge a fee tied to your claim. It gives you the VA's own combined-rating math, the actual text of the 38 CFR criteria, peer-reviewed studies, a back pay estimate, and a directory of free VA-accredited representatives, all without an account.

No single tool or service, AI-based or otherwise, should be your only source on a VA claim. Read the governing regulation, and get a free VA-accredited VSO in your corner before you file.
