Social Security announced a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment on October 14, 2026. VA disability compensation normally rises by the same percentage on December 1. These tables are our estimate of the 2027 amounts, published October 15, 2026.

Estimated. VA has not published its 2027 table, and Congress has not yet passed this year's compensation COLA bill. Every 2027 figure on this page is calculated by VA Ready. Until VA publishes, the 2026 rates on the pay-rate chart are what VA pays.

How we estimated

Each figure is the 2026 amount (effective December 1, 2025) times 1.036, rounded to the nearest cent, or to the nearest dollar where VA publishes whole dollars. We ran the same arithmetic on the 2021 through 2025 tables with each year's COLA. It matched VA's veteran-alone amount for every rating in each of the last five years, and amounts with dependents landed within $1.25. Treat the dependent columns as good to about a dollar.

What happens next

Social Security's increase takes effect on its own. VA disability compensation needs an act of Congress each year. The 2026 bill is S. 4487, the Veterans' Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act of 2026, introduced May 11, 2026 and referred to the Senate Committee on Veterans' Affairs. It sets the same percentage as Social Security, effective December 1, 2026. Once it is signed, VA posts its official table and we will replace these estimates with the published figures.

Questions

Is the 2027 VA disability COLA 3.6 percent?

The Social Security Administration announced a 3.6 percent cost-of-living adjustment on October 14, 2026. VA disability compensation usually rises by the same percentage, but Congress must pass the annual Veterans' Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act first.

When do the 2027 VA disability rates take effect?

The pending bill sets December 1, 2026 as the effective date, the same date as in recent years. VA publishes its official table after the bill becomes law.

How accurate are these 2027 figures?

They are estimates calculated by VA Ready from the 2026 rates and the 3.6 percent COLA. VA's published amounts may differ by up to about a dollar, mostly in the amounts with dependents.
