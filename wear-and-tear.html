Conditions linked to what you did in service

A job code misses most of what service put your body through. These pages start from the activity instead: the rucks, the doors, the jumps, the ranges, the flight deck, the deployment. Each lists the conditions published research links to it, the reason for each link, and the studies behind it.

This page covers 33 activities. The app carries 226, with the records that prove each one and questions for your own statement.
