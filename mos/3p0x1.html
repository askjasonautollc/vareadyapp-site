VA Disability for 3P0X1 (Security Forces)

Air Force 3P0X1 (Security Forces) is a job that typically involved hazardous noise, combat and traumatic stress, heavy loads and impact, and burn pits and airborne hazards. That pattern is why veterans who held it commonly claim conditions like tinnitus, PTSD, back or neck strain, and hearing loss. This page covers the general pattern for the job, not your record.

Typical exposures for 3P0X1

These are the general hazards that came with the job. Your own exposure depends on your units, deployments, and duties.

  • Hazardous noise

    Weapons fire, engines, aircraft, generators, or machinery at levels linked to tinnitus and hearing loss.

  • Combat and traumatic stress

    Firefights, casualties, mass-casualty care, and sustained threat, the kind of experience behind PTSD and depression claims.

  • Heavy loads and impact

    Rucks, body armor, lifting, carrying patients or ammunition, and hard landings that wear on the back, knees, and feet.

  • Burn pits and airborne hazards

    Smoke and fine particulate from open-air burn pits, dust, and sand during Southwest Asia and post-9/11 deployments. About this exposure →

PACT Act presumptives for 3P0X1 veterans

Many Airmen in this job deployed to Southwest Asia or the post-9/11 theater. Under the PACT Act, the presumption is based on where and when you served, not on your job title. It covers Gulf War service on or after August 2, 1990 in places such as Bahrain, Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, and the United Arab Emirates, and post-9/11 service on or after September 11, 2001 in places such as Afghanistan, Djibouti, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Uzbekistan, and Yemen.

If you served in a qualifying location and are later diagnosed, the VA treats certain conditions as presumed service-connected, so you do not have to prove the exposure caused them. The list includes asthma diagnosed after service, chronic bronchitis, COPD, chronic rhinitis, chronic sinusitis, constrictive bronchiolitis, and a range of cancers. Holding this job does not by itself mean you have any of them.

Common questions

Does the VA have a list of conditions for 3P0X1?

No. The VA does not keep a list of disabilities by MOS, AFSC, or rating. It decides each claim on your own service records, medical evidence, and exam. What a job typically exposed people to can help explain how a condition started, which is why knowing the pattern for 3P0X1 Security Forces is useful.

What conditions do 3P0X1 veterans commonly claim?

Veterans who served as Air Force 3P0X1 (Security Forces) commonly claim conditions such as tinnitus, PTSD, back or neck strain, hearing loss, limited knee flexion, and sleep apnea. Each one still needs its own diagnosis and a link to service.

Are 3P0X1 veterans covered by the PACT Act?

The PACT Act presumptions are based on where and when you served, not on your job. If you served in a qualifying Gulf War or post-9/11 location, certain respiratory conditions and cancers are presumed service-connected.
