Questions this answers

How much is VA disability pay at 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, and 100%?
For a veteran with no dependents, 2026 monthly rates effective December 1, 2025, are $180.42 at 10%, $552.47 at 30%, $1,132.90 at 50%, $1,808.45 at 70%, and $3,938.58 at 100%. Rates rise with dependents starting at 30%.Source: Current Veterans disability compensation rates (VA.gov)
At what rating can I get extra pay for dependents?
You can get added compensation for a spouse, children, or dependent parents once your combined rating is at least 30%. At 10% and 20%, you won't receive a higher rate even if you have a dependent spouse, child, or parent.Source: Current Veterans disability compensation rates (VA.gov)
What VA health care priority group am I in with my rating?
A rating of 50% or more, or being found unemployable due to service-connected disability, can place you in priority group 1. A rating of 30% or 40% can place you in group 2, and 10% or 20% can place you in group 3.Source: VA health care priority groups (VA.gov)
Do disabled veterans pay the VA home loan funding fee?
No, if you are receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, or you are eligible for it but receive retirement or active-duty pay instead. If VA later awards compensation with an effective date before your loan closing, you may get a refund.Source: VA funding fee and loan closing costs (VA.gov)
What rating do I need for Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E)?
Veterans need a service-connected disability rating of at least 10% and a discharge that is not dishonorable to apply. Service members may qualify with a memorandum rating of 20% or more before discharge.Source: Veteran Readiness and Employment eligibility (VA.gov)
Do I get VA dental care at 100%?
If you have service-connected disabilities rated 100%, or you are unemployable and paid at the 100% rate, you are in dental Class IV. A temporary 100% rating, such as for a long hospital stay, does not qualify.Source: VA dental care (VA.gov)
Can my family get CHAMPVA health care?
Your spouse or dependent child may qualify for CHAMPVA if VA rated you permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected disability. VA defines permanent and total as rated 100% and not expected to improve.Source: CHAMPVA benefits (VA.gov)
Can my spouse or kids get education benefits from my rating?
The Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance (DEA) program, also called Chapter 35, may cover the child or spouse of a veteran who is permanently and totally disabled due to a service-connected disability.Source: Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance (VA.gov)
