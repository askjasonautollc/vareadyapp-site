How to organize your claim evidence for VA

VA decides on what is in the file. The claim file keeps every record on your device, labeled by document type and tied to the condition it supports. Ready for VA (Pro) renames files with the upload category to pick on VA.gov, and the VSO packet (Pro) puts everything in one PDF with an index.

Where it is in the app: Filing tab, Tools, Claim file. The VSO packet is on the My VSO tab. The claim file is free; Ready for VA and the VSO packet are part of Pro.

Steps

  1. Open the Filing tab and tap Claim file.
  2. Scan or import a record, label its type and pick the condition it supports.
  3. Tap Ready for VA to create renamed copies, each with its VA.gov upload category.
  4. On the My VSO tab, open VSO packet and tap Build the packet.

Questions this answers

What documents do I need for a VA disability claim?
VA needs your DD214 or other separation documents, your service treatment records, and medical evidence related to your condition, such as doctor's reports, X-rays, and test results. You can submit these or give VA permission to gather them.Source: Evidence needed for your disability claim (VA.gov)
How do I get VA to request my private medical records?
Complete VA Form 21-4142 and VA Form 21-4142a to authorize VA to get records from a non-VA provider or Vet Center. Both forms come in the same PDF, and you can submit online or by mail.Source: Evidence needed for your disability claim (VA.gov)
Does VA help me gather evidence?
Yes. VA is required to help you gather evidence to support your claim, which is called the duty to assist.Source: Decision reviews FAQs (VA.gov)
Do I need to request my DD214 before I file?
No. When VA receives your application for benefits, it requests your DD214 for you.Source: Request your military service records (VA.gov)
How do I upload evidence to my VA claim?
If you file online, you can upload evidence during the application. After you file, use the claim status tool. For a decision review or appeal, use the QuickSubmit tool.Source: Upload evidence to support your disability claim (VA.gov)
How long do I have to send evidence after I file?
You can keep uploading documents for up to 1 year from the date VA receives your claim. If you do not send evidence or the information VA needs within 30 days, VA may decide your claim sooner, and you still have the rest of the year to send more.Source: Upload evidence to support your disability claim (VA.gov)
What happens if I add evidence after filing a fully developed claim?
VA removes your claim from the Fully Developed Claims program and processes it as a standard claim.Source: Upload evidence to support your disability claim (VA.gov)
What kinds of records help a VA disability claim?
VA lists military medical and personnel records, Social Security or other federal or state treatment records, National Guard or Reserve records, private records like doctor's reports, X-rays, and lab results, and supporting statements from people who know about your condition.Source: Upload evidence to support your disability claim (VA.gov)
