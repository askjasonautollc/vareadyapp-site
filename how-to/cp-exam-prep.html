How to prepare for your C&P exam

Examiners fill out a set form for each condition. The questionnaire puts those questions in front of you at home, so you can think through onset, frequency, flare-ups and how the condition affects work before you are in the room.

Where it is in the app: My Claim, open a condition, C&P exam questionnaire. Part of VA Ready Pro.

Steps

  1. On My Claim, open the condition with the exam.
  2. Tap C&P exam questionnaire.
  3. Read Why this matters, then answer each question in your own words.
  4. Tap Export my answers as PDF and save or print it.
  5. Bring the printout to the exam and give a copy to your VSO.

Questions this answers

What is a C&P exam?
A compensation and pension (C&P) exam, also called a VA claim exam, helps VA decide whether you have a service-connected disability and, if you do, how to rate it. Your rating affects how much compensation you receive.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
Does everyone who files a VA claim need a C&P exam?
No. VA asks for an exam only when it needs more information. If your file already has enough medical evidence, VA may decide using the Acceptable Clinical Evidence (ACE) process and review your records instead.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
What happens at a C&P exam?
The provider may do a basic physical exam, ask questions from the Disability Benefits Questionnaire (DBQ) for each condition you claimed, and order tests like X-rays or blood work at no cost to you. The provider will not treat you, prescribe medicine, or tell you the results.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
Can my own doctor fill out a DBQ for my VA claim?
Yes. You can have your own provider examine you and complete a DBQ, then submit it to VA. VA will not pay for your provider to complete it.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
What should I bring to my C&P exam?
You do not need to bring anything. If you have new non-VA medical records, submit them before the appointment, for example through the claim status tool. The provider may look at records you bring, but cannot submit them for you.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
How do I reschedule a C&P exam?
Call the number on your appointment letter at least 48 hours ahead. With a contractor exam, you can usually reschedule only once, and the new appointment must be within 5 days of the original one.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
What happens if I miss my C&P exam?
Missing the exam delays your claim, and VA may decide your claim on the evidence it already has. If you had good cause, such as a death in your immediate family, homelessness, hospitalization, or a terminal illness, VA will schedule a new appointment.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
Can VA deny my claim if I miss the exam?
It depends on the claim. If you miss an exam for an original compensation claim without good cause, VA rates the claim on the evidence of record. For a claim for increase or a supplemental claim, the claim is denied.Source: 38 CFR 3.655
Will the examiner test pain in my joints?
The rating schedule says joints should be tested for pain on both active and passive motion, in weight-bearing and nonweight-bearing positions, and if possible compared with the opposite undamaged joint.Source: 38 CFR 4.59
How do I get a copy of my C&P exam results?
You cannot get the results at the exam or from the provider. Request a copy of the final exam report with a Freedom of Information Act or Privacy Act Request, VA Form 20-10206, online, by mail, or in person.Source: VA claim exam (C&P exam) (VA.gov)
