What VA Ready does

VA Ready helps you build a VA disability claim from start to finish: what to claim, how it rates, how to prove it, and where you are in the process. Everything stays on your device. Free covers the rating and the basics; Pro adds the research and the exports.

My Claim and the combined ratingFree

Add each condition and its rating, and VA Ready works out your combined rating the way VA does, step by step, with the bilateral factor, and shows the monthly pay for your dependents.

Each condition keeps its evidence count, exam status and notes on your device.

Claim scenariosPro

Pick a scenario at the top of My Claim to try a different claim. Scenario 1 is your claim. Scenarios 2 to 5 start as a copy or empty, and they never reach your forms, VSO packet or exports.

What-If

Test a possible claim against your rating before you decide what to pursue, with suggestions from your job code, exposures and duties.

Claim Intel: your job code and exposuresPro

Conditions common for your MOS, AFSC or rating, ranked, with PACT Act presumptives and the conditions suggested by your exposures, bases and aircraft. It covers 1,106 job codes across every branch, including legacy codes.

Wear and TearPro

Search 226 things you did in service, from kicking in doors and rucking to flight decks and Camp Lejeune. Each one lists the conditions linked to it, the studies behind each link, the records that prove you did it, and questions for your own statement. Mark what you did and the conditions gather into one list.

Read the conditions and sources for the most searched activities on the Wear and Tear pages.

Top VA claimsFree

The most common conditions VA rates, from VA's 2025 Annual Benefits Report: for all veterans, for veterans newly rated this year, by era, after 9/11, and by body system, with VA's count for each.

What decided Board casesPro

What helped and what hurt in real Board of Veterans' Appeals decisions on your condition, with the decision numbers behind each finding.

C&P exam prepPro

Answer the questions an examiner works from, at home and in your own words, then export them as a PDF to bring with you.

Claim NavigatorPro

Step-by-step directions from where you are in the claim to a decision, with a guide for each step, including increases, TDIU and a proposed reduction.

Claim file and VSO packet

Scan or import your records, label each one with the VA document type, and tie it to the condition it supports. Pro adds a VSO packet with an evidence index on the front page.

Symptom log and buddy statements

Log flare-ups as they happen and build buddy statements from guided templates. Pro adds unlimited history, reminders and PDF exports.

Benefits and finding a VSO

Federal benefits by rating, state benefits for every state with Pro, and a map of VA-accredited VSO offices near you.
